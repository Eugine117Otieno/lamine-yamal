1. His History

Early Life

Lamine Yamal Nasraoui Ebana was born on 13 July 2007 in Esplugues de Llobregat, Spain. He grew up in Mataró, in the Rocafonda neighbourhood.

Childhood and Football

Lamine developed an interest in football at a young age. He joined FC Barcelona's youth academy, La Masia, when he was seven years old.

2. Football Career

Barcelona Career

Lamine made his Barcelona first-team debut in April 2023 at the age of 15. He plays as a forward and is known for his dribbling, speed and creativity.

Spain National Team

Lamine represents Spain internationally. He helped Spain win UEFA Euro 2024.

3. FIFA World Cup 2026

His World Cup Journey

Lamine represented Spain in the 2026 FIFA World Cup, hosted by the United States, Canada and Mexico. Spain went on to win the tournament.
